6. You don't make it out.
7. The only questions are how long you last, and what finally gets you. [CICLO-MESTRE: aberto aqui, fecha na cena 167]
8. And the strangest part is that this place started with a real photo. [O1]
9. It was taken in 2002, inside a store being renovated in Oshkosh, Wisconsin. [O1]
10. In 2019, someone posted it on 4chan with a short story about "six hundred million square miles of randomly segmented empty rooms." [O2]

## 1. Hour 1 (1:10–2:45) · [CONTADOR: "YOUR COUNT: HOUR 1"]

15. It happens the way it often happens: by accident. [W:faq]

17. Level 0. The wiki calls it Threshold. Most people know it as the Lobby. [W:level-0]
18. Yellow wallpaper. Fluorescent lights. And carpet that is always wet. [W:level-0]

20. The second thing is the sound.

22. That part is real, by the way.
23. Old fluorescent lights flicker about a hundred times a second, too fast for you to see. [S1]
24. In a 1989 study in British offices, switching to lights that didn't flicker cut headaches and eyestrain by more than half. [S1]
25. So within an hour, your head hurts.
26. You call out. Nobody answers.
27. And here's the first rule of Level 0.
28. Even if someone had fallen in right beside you, you would never find each other. [W:level-0]
29. You're alone. Completely.
30. So you walk.
31. Every room looks like the back of an office, or a store that was never finished. [W:level-0]
32. Psychologists have a name for why that feels so wrong.
33. In 2022, a study found that places feel most uncanny when they're almost normal, with something in their structure slightly off. [S2]
34. Level 0 is exactly that. Familiar, and wrong, in every direction.

## 2. Hour 9 (2:45–4:15) · [CONTADOR: "HOUR 9"]

47. Then there's the thirst.

49. Some of it is salt water. Some of it is formaldehyde. [W:level-0]
