What would the first year of your life look like if a New York gang recruited you at eighteen? This is a composite built from federal court records, NYPD and city data, and decades of research on gangs, not the life of any one real person.

It is journalism. It does not teach, encourage or glorify anything: every claim about a crime is attributed to a court record or a study, and every act comes with its legal consequence.
