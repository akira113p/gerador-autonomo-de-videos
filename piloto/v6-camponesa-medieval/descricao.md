What would your life be like as a medieval peasant? We live one year in an English village around 1300, following a real tenant from a 1279 survey: three days a week of unpaid work for the lord, a fee to marry off a daughter, a tax on dying, and a court that fines you for brewing ale.

Along the way we test three popular myths (the lord's "right of the first night", the 150-day work year, and "everyone died at thirty"), see what a peasant really owned, and watch what happens when the rain starts in 1315. The Black Death comes later, and it changes everything.
