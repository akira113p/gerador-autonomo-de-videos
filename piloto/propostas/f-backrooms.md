# What Would YOUR Life Be Like in The Backrooms?

Proposta de roteiro do vídeo 14, **aguardando aprovação**. 167 cenas, ~2.050 palavras, ~14 min no ritmo do Last of Us (147 palavras/min). Cenas de mais de 7 s viram duas imagens na produção (~195 imagens).
Espinha (versão 2, 2026-10-19): você cai das paredes da realidade, atravessa o Level 0, é resgatado pela M.E.G. no Level 1 e, meses depois, sai numa expedição de dois níveis: Level 2 e **Level 3 "Electrical Station"**, onde a equipe cai numa emboscada a 40 jardas da Base Gamma e você morre. Narração mais explícita. Nível escolhido em [../../pesquisa/pesquisa-v14-backrooms-final.md](../../pesquisa/pesquisa-v14-backrooms-final.md). A morte é anunciada aos 25 s (recomendação de `pesquisa/retencao-principios.md`, seção 4.M): a pergunta deixa de ser "sobrevive?" e passa a ser "quanto tempo e o quê te pega".
Base de ficção: Backrooms Wiki do Wikidot (CC BY-SA 3.0), pesquisa em [../../pesquisa/pesquisa-v14-backrooms.md](../../pesquisa/pesquisa-v14-backrooms.md).

Marcadores: **[W:página]** = regra do wiki (página em `backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/<página>`); **[S#]** = ciência real (tabela no fim); **[O#]** = origem real das Backrooms; **[nosso]** = invenção da nossa história, coerente com o wiki mas não escrita nele.
Retenção: **[CICLO]** = pergunta aberta e onde fecha; **[REGANCHO]** = virada a cada ~3 min; **[MÚSICA]** = troca de trilha; **[CONTADOR]** = cartão desenhado em código com a contagem de dias "do seu jeito" (o número fica cada vez menos confiável, e isso paga no fim).

## 0. Abertura (0:00–1:10) · [MÚSICA: ambiente liminar, baixo]

1. You just fell out of reality, into the Backrooms, and the first thing that can kill you here isn't a monster. [W:level-0]
2. It's thirst. [W:level-0]
3. No map. No exit sign. Just damp carpet, yellow walls, and a hum that never stops. [W:level-0]
4. So I'm going to tell you how this goes, from your first step to your last.
5. Because I'll be honest with you from the start.
6. You don't make it out.
7. The only questions are how long you last, and what finally gets you. [CICLO-MESTRE: aberto aqui, fecha na cena 167]
8. And the strangest part is that this place started with a real photo. [O1]
9. It was taken in 2002, inside a store being renovated in Oshkosh, Wisconsin. [O1]
10. In 2019, someone posted it on 4chan with a short story about "six hundred million square miles of randomly segmented empty rooms." [O2]
11. Nobody knew where the photo came from until May 2024. [O1]
12. Since then, writers on the Backrooms Wiki have mapped that place, level by level. [W:licensing-guide]
13. That wiki is our map today. Every rule in this story comes from it.
14. Let's start with the hour you arrive.

## 1. Hour 1 (1:10–2:45) · [CONTADOR: "YOUR COUNT: HOUR 1"]

15. It happens the way it often happens: by accident. [W:faq]
16. You trip in a dark hallway at home, and instead of hitting the floor, you go through it. [nosso, a partir de W:faq]
17. Level 0. The wiki calls it Threshold. Most people know it as the Lobby. [W:level-0]
18. Yellow wallpaper. Fluorescent lights. And carpet that is always wet. [W:level-0]
19. The smell is the first thing that tells you something is wrong. Old, soaked carpet. [W:level-0]
20. The second thing is the sound.
21. The lights hum, and the hum rises and falls, but it never stops. [W:level-0]
22. That part is real, by the way.
23. Old fluorescent lights flicker about a hundred times a second, too fast for you to see. [S1]
24. In a 1989 study in British offices, switching to lights that didn't flicker cut headaches and eyestrain by more than half. [S1]
25. So within an hour, your head hurts.
26. You call out. Nobody answers.
27. And here's the first rule of Level 0.
28. Even if someone had fallen in right beside you, you would never find each other. [W:level-0]
29. You're alone. Completely.
30. So you walk.
31. Every room looks like the back of an office, or a store that was never finished. [W:level-0]
32. Psychologists have a name for why that feels so wrong.
33. In 2022, a study found that places feel most uncanny when they're almost normal, with something in their structure slightly off. [S2]
34. Level 0 is exactly that. Familiar, and wrong, in every direction.

## 2. Hour 9 (2:45–4:15) · [CONTADOR: "HOUR 9"]

35. By hour nine, you notice something worse.
36. You've seen this room before. That stain. That corner.
37. You're walking in circles. [REGANCHO ~2:50]
38. That isn't just the Backrooms. That's you.
39. In 2009, German researchers sent people walking for hours through a forest and across the Sahara, tracked by GPS. [S3]
40. When they could see the sun, they drifted, but kept going straight. [S3]
41. When clouds covered it, they walked in circles. [S3]
42. Blindfolded, some walked circles less than twenty meters wide. [S3]
43. In Level 0, there is no sun.
44. And there's something else. The wiki calls it the Peripheral Shift. [W:level-0]
45. When you're not looking, the rooms rearrange themselves, sometimes erasing days of hallways you've already walked. [W:level-0]
46. So the map in your head is useless.
47. Then there's the thirst.
48. The carpet is wet, but what soaks it is nothing you should drink. [W:level-0]
49. Some of it is salt water. Some of it is formaldehyde. [W:level-0]
50. Real research shows that losing just one or two percent of your body weight in water already hurts your mood and focus, and gives you headaches. [S4]
51. And in Level 0, the wiki says, most lives end exactly this way: dehydration, or starvation. [W:level-0]
52. Then, somewhere to your left, a wall flickers. [CICLO menor: a parede que pisca, fecha na cena 58] [REGANCHO ~4:00]

## 3. Day 3? (4:15–4:55) · [CONTADOR: "DAY 3?"]

53. You don't know how long you've been here. Your phone died on day one. Or what you think was day one.
54. In 1962, a French scientist named Michel Siffre lived alone in a cave for about two months, with no clock and no sunlight. [S5]
55. When they told him it was over, he thought it was the twentieth of August. [S5]
56. It was the middle of September. He had lost almost a month. [S5]
57. Keep that in mind. Your count is already wrong. [CICLO: o contador, fecha na coda, cenas 170–171]
58. The flickering wall is the way out. You throw yourself through it. [W:level-0]
59. If you want the next story like this one, that's what the subscribe button is for. Now, Level 1. [pedido de inscrição, ~34% do vídeo]

## 4. Level 1 (4:55–7:00) · [CONTADOR: "DAY 4?"]

60. Level 1. The wiki calls it the Habitable Zone. [W:level-1]
61. It's an endless underground parking garage crossed with an abandoned warehouse. Concrete, pillars, pipes. [W:level-1]
62. And it has the two things Level 0 didn't: supplies, and people. [W:level-1]
63. Wooden crates appear out of nowhere, often right behind your back. [W:level-1]
64. Inside: food, clothes, tools. And metal bottles. [W:level-1]
65. Almond Water. [W:object-1]
66. According to the wiki, one full bottle is worth a healthy six-hundred-calorie meal. Three a day keep you alive. [W:object-1]
67. One rule: never drink from a bottle that isn't full. Someone else had it first. [W:object-1]
68. You sleep in one of the small rooms off the corridors, with furniture pushed against the door. [W:level-1]
69. But you never lock it. Locks here have a way of disappearing, and people have died trapped inside. [W:level-1]
70. On what you think is day five, the lights go out. [W:level-1] [MÚSICA: tensa]
71. The wiki calls it Flickering, and says it might be one of the deadliest things in the Backrooms. [W:level-1]
72. In the dark, things come out. [W:level-1]
73. The rule is simple: don't turn on your flashlight. [W:level-1]
74. Being the only light in the area, the wiki says, leads to a quick death. [W:level-1]
75. So you sit in the dark, and you listen.
76. Something walks past your door. Then it stops.
77. And a human voice says your name. [W:entity-10] [REGANCHO ~6:00]
78. Hold on to that moment. This exact trick comes back at the end of your story. [CICLO: a voz que imita, fecha nas cenas 124–131 e 165–167]
79. You don't answer. The lights come back. [MÚSICA: volta o ambiente liminar]
80. In the morning, you find out what it was.
81. The wiki calls them Skin-Stealers: tall, pale things that wear the skin of the people they catch. [W:entity-10]
82. They copy voices without understanding the words. [W:entity-10]
83. Survivors have a rhyme for them: "Blood runs red, they're not dead. Blood runs clear, don't get near." [W:entity-10]
84. The people who teach you that rhyme wear lime-yellow, with an eagle on the shoulder. [W:level-1]
85. The M.E.G. The Major Explorer Group. [W:the-m-e-g]
86. You've been found.

## 5. Week 3 (7:00–8:20) · [MÚSICA: jazz] · [CONTADOR: "DAY 21?"]

87. Base Alpha is the oldest M.E.G. base, built in the corridors of Level 1. [W:base-alpha]
88. Newcomers take a test, and then get a job: exploration, archives, research or maintenance. [W:base-alpha]
89. You end up in exploration. Of course you do.
90. There's a diner. People trade Almond Water like money. [W:level-1, W:object-1]
91. For a few weeks, life is almost normal. Shifts, meals, sleep.
92. Your partner on the team is a woman who's been down here for six years. She teaches you how to hold a rifle without shaking. [nosso]
93. And she teaches you the rules the veterans never joke about.
94. One: if you see party decorations, or a poster that says "Time 4 Fun", turn around. [W:entity-67]
95. Two: never trust a voice. Trust blood. [W:entity-10]
96. At the gate of Base Gamma, the big M.E.G. base on Level 3, a guard pricks the finger of everyone who walks in, to check that their blood is red. [W:level-3] [CICLO: o furo no dedo, fecha nas cenas 141–142]
97. The wiki says no exceptions are ever made. Not even for the people who run the M.E.G. [W:level-3]
98. Three: Level 3 is the one place nobody jokes about at all. [W:level-3] [CICLO: o Level 3]
99. Even the M.E.G. calls traveling there "extremely ill-advised." [W:level-3]
100. Twenty percent of its entire budget goes to one thing: keeping Base Gamma alive. [W:level-3]

## 6. Day 200? (8:20–9:20) · [MÚSICA: tensa] · [CONTADOR: "DAY 200?"]

101. Months pass. Your count says day two hundred. You don't trust it anymore.
102. Then your team gets its first long expedition: carry medicine and data from Base Alpha to Base Gamma. [W:base-alpha; missão = nosso]
103. Two levels. Level 2, then Level 3. [W:level-1, W:level-3]
104. Eight of you. Rifles, flares, and a crate of supplies. [nosso]
105. Level 2 first. Maintenance tunnels. Pipes everywhere. Machines humming with no purpose. [W:level-2]
106. Every turn is exactly forty-five degrees. [W:level-2]
107. The lights are wired together, so when one fails, a whole grid of them goes dark. [W:level-2]
108. In the dark parts, there are Smilers. You never see a body. Just a long smile full of teeth, and two white eyes. [W:entity-3]
109. So you do what you were taught. Light off. Eye contact. Walk away slowly. [W:entity-3]
110. At every corner, you go silent, because of the Clumps: piles of limbs that wait in tight corridors. Within eight feet, they reach. [W:entity-5]
111. Nobody dies on Level 2. For a moment, you think the hard part is over. [REGANCHO ~9:00]
112. Then you reach the door. [W:level-3]
113. Most of the doors from Level 2 into Level 3 are barricaded, or covered in warning signs. This one has both. And a gap. [W:level-3]
114. Psychologists say walking through a doorway makes people forget what they were just holding in mind. [S6]
115. Remember that. On the other side, forgetting one small rule is all it takes.

## 7. Level 3 (9:20–11:00) · [MÚSICA: tensa, mais forte] · virada em ~66%

116. Level 3. The Electrical Station. [W:level-3]
117. Brick walls in mismatched colors. Dusty concrete. Weak fluorescent lights. Cables and fuse boxes everywhere. [W:level-3]
118. Pipes full of a thick black liquid. Some corridors are barely wider than your shoulders. [W:level-3]
119. And metal bars across most of the hallways. Bars nobody has ever managed to break. [W:level-3]
120. The danger here isn't one monster. It's what the monsters do together. [W:level-3]
121. The wiki says the entities on Level 3 attack in "military-esque brigades", with careful planning, and a better map of the level than yours. [W:level-3]
122. Seeing one entity wandering by itself almost never happens. [W:level-3]
123. And sometimes, those brigades lead a wanderer, on purpose, into a dead end. [W:level-3]
124. Two hours in, you meet a man in a hooded coat and a mask. [W:level-3]
125. He says he's lost. He has a thick accent. He's limping. He asks for water. [W:level-3]
126. You're tired. He's human. Nobody asks to see his blood. [CICLO: o furo no dedo]
127. A hundred yards later, there's a dim light at the end of the corridor. No lamp. No source. [W:level-3]

## 8. The bars (11:00–12:45) · [MÚSICA: tensa no máximo, depois corta]

139. The corridor ends in metal bars.
140. And through the bars, maybe forty yards away, you can see the lights of Base Gamma. [nosso]
141. Guards. Sandbags. A gate. A man with a needle, ready to prick your finger. [W:level-3] [CICLO: o furo no dedo fecha]
142. If anyone had pricked one finger two hours ago, you'd be standing over there. [nosso]
143. Your team turns around and fights.
144. Clumps eight feet tall, holding weapons. Facelings with brick shields and blades. [W:level-3]
145. And somewhere behind them, giving orders, a Death Rat. The wiki calls them the leaders of these operations. [W:level-3]
146. Rifles. Flares. Your partner throws Firesalt, and for a moment the corridor burns white. [W:level-3]
147. Then the Deathmoths come in. [W:level-3]
148. The wiki says a swarm goes for the mouth and the lungs, and can kill in a matter of seconds. [W:level-3]
149. Your partner goes down first. You hear her stop breathing. [nosso]
150. Then the others. One by one.
151. You're the last one, back against the bars, out of ammunition.
152. The Hounds on Level 3 don't kill quickly. [W:level-3]
153. The wiki says they try to make it as slow and as painful as possible. [W:level-3]
154. One bites the back of your neck. Not enough to kill you. Just enough so you can't move. [W:level-3]
155. You open your mouth to scream for the guards at the gate. [W:level-3]
156. The second Hound takes your voice before you can. [W:level-3]
157. The lights of Base Gamma are still on. Forty yards. [CICLO-MESTRE fecha] [MÚSICA: corta; 2 s de silêncio]
